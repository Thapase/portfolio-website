# T.H.H. Thapase — Personal & Professional Portfolio Website

An executive, high-performance portfolio website built with modern HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript with zero build dependencies. Designed for **T.H.H. Thapase**, Supply Chain & Strategy Consultant at Genpact, IIM Sirmaur MBA alumnus (GMAT 760), endurance athlete, and ESG advocate.

---

## 🚀 Quick Launch
You do not need Node.js, npm, or any server setup. 
Simply **double-click `index.html`** in this folder to open the website instantly in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

---

## 🎨 Key Features

1. **Bespoke Day/Dark Mode Switcher**:
   - **Dark Mode ("Obsidian Velocity")**: High-contrast slate/obsidian (`#0B0F19`) with Emerald (`#10B981`) and Strava Amber (`#F97316`) accents.
   - **Light Mode ("Alabaster Precision")**: Clean porcelain slate (`#F8FAFC`) with crisp typography and deep emerald/amber highlights.
   - Persists automatically across browser sessions via `localStorage`.

2. **Supply Chain Logistics Network Animation**:
   - An interactive HTML5 Canvas layer rendering transit nodes (plants, ports, distribution hubs) and dynamic moving shipment packets that interact smoothly with your cursor.

3. **Client Confidentiality & Impact Showcase**:
   - Anonymized case studies safeguarding Fortune 500 proprietary client data while spotlighting quantified business outcomes ($30M+ savings, 12 → 3 weeks cycle time, 20-30% OTIF improvement, $2M circular economy ESG savings).
   - Interactive category filter tabs (Cycle Time, ML/Predictive, ESG & Sustainability, Power BI).

4. **The Endurance & Beyond Work Dimension**:
   - Strava-integrated running & endurance philosophy card.
   - Interactive 15+ Indian States & Union Territories travel cloud.
   - Cricket & Badminton agility cards.

5. **Life Journey & Academic Milestones**:
   - Chronological timeline featuring SRM University (EEE 88% Distinction), IIM Sirmaur MBA (GMAT 760), and Genpact consulting leadership.

6. **Social Impact & 1:1 Mentorship**:
   - Spotlighting the 320 girl-child education initiative, Goonj flood relief, and Etherisc farmer carbon credits.
   - Direct integration with your verified Topmate profile (`@thapase_thh`) for 1:1 consulting and GMAT mentorship.

7. **Sanitized Curriculum Vitae (CV) Modal**:
   - Click the "Resume" button anywhere on the page to open a formatted view with a one-click Print / Save as PDF option.

## 📸 Adding Your Photos (Google Photos / Local Photos)

To display your actual pictures on the site:
1. Download 2–5 favorite photos from Google Photos or your computer.
2. Place them into:
   ```
   assets/images/
   ├── avatar.jpg    (Your profile headshot)
   ├── running.jpg   (A race/running photo)
   └── travel.jpg    (A favorite travel photo)
   ```
3. If no images are provided, the site gracefully renders bespoke monogram badges and iconography.

## ✏️ Updating Your Information

All website text, metrics, links, case studies, and hobbies are centralized in:
`js/config.js`

You can open `js/config.js` in any text editor (Cursor, VS Code, or Notepad) and update any value without touching HTML markup.
